Blinkit · Loyalty & Pricing

Surge Fee → Surge Coins

A loyalty-loop model that reduces surge-pricing friction and improves GMV. The surge fee stays and the revenue stays — but 40% returns as time-bound coins, so the moment that today pushes a customer toward a competitor becomes the reason they come back. It only profits where those coins cause genuinely new orders, which is what the model tests.

How I ThinkAbout Product

Frameworks are only worth naming if they changed a decision. These are the ones that actually shaped what got built — and what got deliberately parked.

01

Pick one metric

A case study optimising three things optimises nothing. One north star, everything else demoted to supporting or guardrail.

02

Name the failure condition

Every model should say when it stops working. Break-even lines and kill-conditions are the argument, not a disclaimer.

03

Protect the existing business

New features that quietly cannibalise revenue don't ship. Additive beats disruptive when the incumbent is already working.

04

Design for the whole market

India is not one language or one income. Pan-India products need mechanics that degrade gracefully, not assumptions that exclude.
